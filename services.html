Adoption Process

  1. Browse our available animals online or visit the shelter during open hours.
  2. Complete a short adoption application so we can learn about your home.
  3. Meet your chosen pet and talk with our adoption team about its needs.
  4. Finalize your adoption and take home a pet with a starter care plan.

Foster Program

Fostering gives an animal a calm place to recover until it is ready for adoption. We provide food, supplies, and veterinary care. You provide the love and the home. Foster placements last from a few days to several weeks, and our team is available around the clock for questions.

Volunteer Roles

Shelter Care Assistant

Feed, walk, and groom animals, and help keep kennels clean and safe. Shifts are about three hours.

Adoption Event Helper

Greet visitors, share pet information, and help match families with animals at weekend events.

Transport Driver

Pick up animals from partner clinics and deliver them to foster homes or veterinary appointments.

Administrative Volunteer

Support intake, update adoption records, and help with social media and newsletters.
