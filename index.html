Our Mission

We rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in our community. Every animal deserves a safe place to recover and a family that will love it. We work alongside volunteers, fosters, and donors to give each animal a second chance at a happy life.

Our Impact

  • More than 1,200 animals rescued since we opened our doors.
  • Over 950 animals placed in loving adoptive homes.
  • 340 active volunteers and foster caregivers.
  • Nearly 80 percent of our operating support comes from local donors.
